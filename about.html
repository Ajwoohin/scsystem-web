Why SCsystem was created

SCsystem started with observing everyday challenges at Saint Columban College, especially the uncertainty students can face when looking for updates about campus buses. Even small worries about transportation and campus information can distract students from what matters most: learning and enjoying their school experience.

The goal is to bring useful campus updates and student activities into one place, making information easier to find and helping students feel more prepared and connected. We want students to spend less time worrying about the little things and more time focusing on their studies, their growth, and campus life.
